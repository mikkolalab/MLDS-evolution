### Creat seurat object and QC
Seurat objects were created using Seurat v.5.3.0. Cells with fewer than 500 or more than 7,500 detected genes, or with more than 15% mitochondrial transcripts were excluded. Putative doublets were identified with DoubletFinder78 v2.0.4 and removed. Counts were log-normalized with NormalizeData, highly variable features were identified with FindVariableFeatures, and data were scaled with ScaleData after regression of total UMI counts (nCount_RNA). Principal component analysis (PCA) was performed on the scaled data. A shared-nearest-neighbour graph was constructed with FindNeighbors using the first 15 principal components (TAM and MLDS dataset, Fig.1, Fig.3, and Fig.4) or the first the 12 principal components (fetal tissue and selected TAM and MLDS dataset, Fig.2), and clusters were identified with FindClusters at a resolution of 0.5. UMAP was used for two-dimensional visualization. Sample objects were merged without batch correction, and the merged expression matrix was used for dimensionality reduction and UMAP visualization.
